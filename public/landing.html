FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to be technical to set this up?
Not at all! If you can use WhatsApp, you can set this up. Our guided wizard walks you through every step. Most businesses are live in under 5 minutes.
Will the bot use my WhatsApp number?
Yes! You scan a QR code with your own WhatsApp Business number. Your customers message YOUR number and your bot replies from YOUR number.
Does it understand Pidgin English?
Yes! Messages like "una dey open?", "how much e go cost?", "abeg wia una dey?" are all handled correctly.
What if a customer asks something the bot doesn't know?
The bot sends a helpful fallback message showing customers what questions it can answer. You can see all conversations in your dashboard and follow up manually.
Can I change my prices and FAQs anytime?
Yes! Log into your dashboard anytime to update your prices, hours, FAQs or greeting message. Changes take effect immediately.
